# CropLab v1.4 — Editing Engine & Interaction UX

## Crop
- Free crop and visual aspect-ratio presets.
- Pixel crop dimensions.
- Crop reset restores crop position/zoom without removing rotation/flip.
- Cropper interactions participate in history.

## Resize
- Explicit width/height pixel properties.
- Aspect ratio locked by default.
- Width immediately derives height and vice versa.
- Independent dimensions when unlocked.

## Rotation
- Persistent 90° left/right controls.
- -180° to 180° slider.
- Rotation participates in history.

## Flip
- Icon-first horizontal/vertical controls.
- v1.1 full-transform flip fix preserved.

## Zoom
- 20%–200%.
- Minus/plus controls.
- Fit, 100%, 200%, Reset.
- Mouse-wheel and touch pinch.
- Keyboard zoom shortcuts `+`, `=`, `-`, `0`, `1`, `2`.
- Cropper keyboard movement preserved.

## Verification
Typecheck, lint, tests, build, startup, and browser QA covered crop, ratios, reset, linked dimensions, rotation, flip, zoom, history, and export.

## Freeze
v1.4 was frozen as the editing-engine baseline.
